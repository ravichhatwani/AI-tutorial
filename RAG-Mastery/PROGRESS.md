## Realistic calendar (about 58 days)
2 hours a day is tight, so every 7th day is a **buffer day**: finish unfinished work, redo a weak topic, or rest. Days marked HEAVY in the phase files (3, 12, 13, 29, 33, 34, 47) may need 2 sessions; the next buffer day absorbs the overflow.

| Week | Study days (plan Day #) | Buffer (calendar day) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1-6 | 7 |
| 2 | 7-12 | 14 |
| 3 | 13-18 | 21 |
| 4 | 19-24 | 28 |
| 5 | 25-30 | 35 |
| 6 | 31-36 | 42 |
| 7 | 37-42 | 49 |
| 8 | 43-48 | 56 |
| 9 | 49-50 | 57-58 final review |

Example (problem): Day 12 (HNSW + FAISS) takes 3.5 hours and you fall behind.
Example (fix): finish it in session 2 on the week's buffer day; no later day is shifted.
